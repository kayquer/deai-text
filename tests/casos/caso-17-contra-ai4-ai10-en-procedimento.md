# caso: contra-teste AI-4 e AI-10 em EN — uniformidade legítima de procedimento, tríade real

## entrada
Before opening the panel, confirm the system is at atmospheric pressure.

1. Close the main valve at the wall.
2. Open the bleed valve and wait for the gauge to read zero.
3. Lock out the breaker and tag it with your name and the date.
4. Remove the four M6 bolts on the front cover.

The indicator has three states: red, amber, and green. Red means the interlock is engaged. Amber means the bleed is still open. Green means the panel is safe to remove.
